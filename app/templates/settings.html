Publishing: manual workflow (current MVP)

The app does not contact Pinterest. It prepares drafts: you download the image, copy the title, description and affiliate link from the Draft Library, post on Pinterest yourself and mark the draft as published.

The official-API integration (OAuth, boards, publishing, scheduling, analytics sync) is built, tested and isolated behind DIRECT_PUBLISHING_ENABLED. When your Pinterest app has the access you need, set DIRECT_PUBLISHING_ENABLED=true in .env and follow engine-docs/PINTEREST_SETUP.md. Until then no Pinterest request can be made.
