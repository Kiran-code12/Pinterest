The best sunscreen is the one you enjoy wearing every day. If it feels greasy or leaves a grey cast, you will skip it. Here is what to check before you buy.

## Look for these on the label

- **SPF 30 or higher**, and **broad spectrum** so it covers both UVA and UVB rays.
- A texture you like: gel, fluid, lotion or stick. Gels and fluids tend to suit oily skin.
- **No white cast** claims, which often come from newer filters or tinted formulas. Check reviews from people with your skin tone.

## How much to apply

Most guidance suggests about two finger lengths for your face and neck. Apply it as the last step of your morning routine, and reapply if you are outside for long periods.

## Make it a habit

- Keep one by your bed, one in your bag.
- Apply it even on cloudy days and when you are indoors near windows.
- Wear it on your ears, neck and the back of your hands too.

A dermatologist can recommend a sunscreen if you have sensitive skin or a skin condition.
