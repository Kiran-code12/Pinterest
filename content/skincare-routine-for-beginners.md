Starting skincare can feel overwhelming, but a routine that you actually follow beats a shelf full of products. Begin with three basics and add more only when your skin is comfortable.

## Morning routine

- **Cleanse** with a gentle face wash, or just rinse with water if your skin is dry.
- **Moisturize** with a lightweight cream or gel, even if your skin is oily.
- **Sunscreen** as the final step. Reapply every two to three hours if you are outdoors.

## Night routine

- **Cleanse** to remove sunscreen, oil and dirt from the day.
- **Moisturize** to help your skin barrier recover overnight.

## When to add a serum

Once your basics feel easy for a few weeks, you can add one serum at a time. A vitamin C serum is a popular morning option, while niacinamide works in the morning or at night. Introduce only one new product every two weeks so you can tell what your skin likes.

## Tips that save money

- Patch test new products on your inner arm for 24 hours.
- Sunscreen matters more than any serum.
- Be patient. Most products need around four to eight weeks to show any difference.

If you have persistent acne, irritation or a skin condition, check with a dermatologist before changing your routine.
