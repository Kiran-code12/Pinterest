Both ingredients are popular in skincare, and both are well liked for helping skin look brighter and more even over time. They do slightly different jobs, though.

## Vitamin C

- An antioxidant, usually used in the **morning** under sunscreen.
- Commonly used for dullness and an uneven-looking skin tone.
- Can sting on sensitive skin, so start with a low strength.

## Niacinamide

- Used in the **morning or at night**.
- Commonly used for oil balance, the look of pores and uneven tone.
- Usually well tolerated, though high strengths can irritate some people.

## Can you use both?

Many people do. A simple way is vitamin C in the morning and niacinamide at night, or niacinamide under vitamin C if your skin handles it. Start with one, wait a couple of weeks, then add the other.

## Quick safety checklist

- Patch test first.
- Always finish your morning routine with sunscreen.
- Stop using a product if it burns or causes a rash, and ask a dermatologist if it continues.

Results vary from person to person, and no serum works overnight.
